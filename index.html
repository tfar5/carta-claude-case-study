Launching Carta Plugins for Claude

In April 2026, Carta launched three Claude plugins: Cap Table, Investors (Fund Admin) and CRM. As Senior Product Marketing Manager, Todd Farnham led the go-to-market across Product, Creative and Sales, defining research-backed positioning for VC, PE and Fund Admin clients and running a three-phase rollout: a soft launch in April, a main launch in June and a webinar launch in July. He produced the launch creative, including 16 skill videos, 5 internal sales decks and 7 internal interactive demos, and built the Claude-based sales enablement skills from scratch. The results: more than a quarter of Carta's 6,000+ clients adopted the plugins within a month of the main launch, 34% of webinar registrants downloaded a plugin within a week, and 88% of sales reps adopted the new tools.
